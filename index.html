About & disclaimer

Unofficial and independent

The CCAR-F Field Guide is an independent study aid made by and for a study group. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or approved by Anthropic, PBC. "Anthropic", "Claude", "Claude Code" and "Claude Certified Architect" are names and trademarks of Anthropic, used here only to identify the exam this guide helps people prepare for.

What's here

The study plan lists the exam's five domains and their task statements. Each task statement has a plain-language summary; its decisions (the usual answer for each symptom a question describes) followed by the facts & terms to recall; and its exam traps, beliefs that sound right but aren't. Every row has an Arabic translation one tap away (the ع button), and a bookmark button that saves it to your Bookmarks (kept in this browser).

Practice test (in the header, and beside each task's Practice Qs tick) opens CertSafari's practice quiz in a new tab. It's an external site that this guide doesn't run or vouch for.

No real exam questions

Nothing on this site comes from the live exam. It contains no exam questions, answers or recalled items, and it should never be used to share them. Candidates are bound by the exam's confidentiality terms.

How to use it

The exam presents varied scenarios that need reasoning. Use the decisions to check your reasoning, not as keyword lookups: the details of the scenario decide. Read the summary first to understand why each mechanism works the way it does, and use quiz mode on the decisions, facts & terms and traps to test yourself.

Accuracy

  • The content was drafted with AI assistance and may contain mistakes or omissions.
  • Claude, Claude Code, the Agent SDK and MCP change quickly. Flags, field names, limits and model behaviour described here may be out of date. Check the official Anthropic documentation before relying on any detail.

No guarantee

Using this guide does not guarantee a passing score. Treat it as a review aid alongside the official documentation and hands-on practice.

Use

This guide is for our study group's own use. Please don't sell it or present it as official material. If you spot a mistake, tell the maintainer so it can be fixed for everyone.
